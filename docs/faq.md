# FAQ

## Why are there no LaTeX files here?

This release is intentionally code-and-results only. Manuscript sources were excluded to keep the
repository focused and lightweight.

## Why are some heavy `.pt` files missing?

Large checkpoints, activity tensors, and rewired-mask payloads make the repository much heavier
without helping most readers inspect the corrected claim. The bundle keeps the summary tables and
figures instead, plus the scripts needed to regenerate the excluded files when external assets are
available.

## Why does the corrected claim sound negative?

Because the strongest honest conclusion is negative or at least sharply qualified: the early apparent
topology advantage is not robust to better controls.

## Are the exploratory structured-null experiments hidden?

No. They are placed under [`archive/`](../archive) and clearly labeled as exploratory, failed, or
inconclusive branches.
